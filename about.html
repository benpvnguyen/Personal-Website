Background

I study Mathematical Physics at the University of Waterloo with a minor in Computing. I'm drawn to mathematical modelling, analytical reasoning and problem-solving, and I enjoy building, testing and improving systems where theory meets real-world challenges.

That mix shows up in my work: I've built Power BI dashboards and Microsoft Copilot agents as a Business Technology Analyst co-op at Manulife and John Hancock, developed React interfaces for drone telemetry with the Waterloo Aerial Robotics Group (WARG), and modelled data in Python and SQL.

Alongside the technical side, running an independent eBay business and working part-time at Tim Hortons taught me clear communication, time management, attention to detail and accountability in fast-paced, customer-facing environments.
